Sort porn videos into folders with the keyboard: one key per category

Thousands of videos downloaded over the years, dumped in "Downloads" or on an external drive, with names that say nothing: sorting them one by one with the mouse never ends. The method that works fits in one idea: one decision, one key, and the next video right away.

In short

Create a dozen categories (your destination folders), give each one a key, then go through the videos one by one. For each, press its category's key, or Del, or Space to leave it. Judge from previews instead of opening every video.

That's exactly what Prisme does, and it's how you sort in one evening what had been piling up for months.

1. Choose your categories

The right number is the one you remember without thinking: six to fifteen to start. A few principles:

  • One category per way you search: by genre, by favorite performer or studio, by series, whatever matches your habits.
  • A "To review" folder for hesitations: it keeps you from getting stuck on one video.
  • A "Favorites" folder is pointless if your tool handles favorites separately: a video can be filed and starred at the same time.
  • The same categories everywhere: PC, external drive and NAS, so an "Amateur" folder doesn't end up existing three times.

2. Judge without opening

The time you lose isn't in filing, it's in opening: launch the video, skip ahead to see, close. A preview of several moments of the video is almost always enough to decide. It also reveals failed, cut or duplicate videos, to delete right away.

3. One key, then the next

Every extra gesture is paid thousands of times. With the mouse, filing a file takes five gestures: right-click, cut, find the folder, paste, go back. With the keyboard, one. And the next video must show up immediately, with the copy happening in the background.

What Prisme does

Prisme is Windows software to sort, organize and watch an adult video collection, built around that gesture.

  1. Point it at your folder. Downloads, an external drive, a NAS, or several at once: Prisme combines them. It scans in the background, and you start right away.
  2. Declare your categories. Each destination folder gets a key: 6 7 8 9, then letters, up to thirty. Digits 0 to 5 are kept for favorites: one hand rates, the other files.
  3. Sort. The video plays large, with five moments lined up beside it; hold Shift to see nine. A key files it, Del deletes it, Space moves to the next, 1 stars it. The next one shows up instantly; the move happens in the background.
  4. A mistake? Ctrl+Z undoes the last action. Deleted files wait in the session trash.
  • With the mouse: right-click opens your first nine categories in a circle around the pointer, without stopping the video. And Ultra sort: left-click keeps, right-click deletes.
  • Hover over a folder: ten previews of its videos play, to sort whole folders.
  • "Unseen" keeps only what you haven't sorted yet; random order shows what you've never watched first.
  • Automatic keywords group videos by the words in their names, for batch sorting (details).
  • Never overwrites: a file with the same name gets (2), (3)… When closing, Prisme waits for every copy to arrive.
  • Discreet: Ctrl+K hides everything behind a dull screen (details).
What Prisme doesn't do
  • It doesn't sort for you: it makes every decision instant.
  • It doesn't fetch metadata online (performers, studios): organization happens in your folders, with your categories.

Frequently asked questions

How should I organize my porn collection?

In a dozen categories that match how you search (genre, performer, series…), plus a "To review" folder. Then one decision per video, with the keyboard, judging from previews.

How long does it take to sort a big collection?

With the mouse, weeks. With keyboard sorting and previews, a decision takes a second or two: a few evenings for several thousand videos.

Does Prisme change my files?

Only when you ask: move, rename, delete. Favorites, keywords and the index are stored separately, and every action can be undone.
