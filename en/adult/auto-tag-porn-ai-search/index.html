Auto-tag porn videos and find a scene with local AI

Nobody tags thousands of videos by hand. Yet file names already hold most of what you need, and an AI running on your own PC can recognize what a picture shows. Here is how to use both to find any video, without sending anything online.

In short

Prisme turns the words in your file names into categories automatically, without typing anything. It recognizes files from the same series by their names, and lets you add your own keywords without moving a single file.

Its AI Lab, in beta, finds a scene from a description, with a model that runs entirely on your computer.

Automatic keywords

  • The most frequent words in your file names become categories, from the fullest to the rarest.
  • Glued-together names are split at capitals and digits, so a name written as one block still yields its words one by one.
  • Noise is filtered out: common French and English words, and file jargon (1080p, x264, web…).
  • One video, one category: the one whose word describes it best. You don't open ten categories to find the same ten videos.
  • Case and accents ignored.
  • Your own keywords, multi-word phrases included, matched in file names. A view, like everything else: nothing moves on disk.
  • "Names with no words": files with unreadable names (0x56b47…, IMG_2041) are gathered apart, to be watched and filed.

Series, recognized by their names

Files from the same series often share an exact piece of name, at the start or in the middle, while the rest changes. Prisme spots them in seconds, even across tens of thousands of files. You can then put them on the Wall, browse them like a folder, make a group without moving anything, or file them in one gesture into a real folder named after them.

Search by description, with local AI

  • Describe the scene, in English or French. Prisme shows the closest videos, each at its best moment; a double-click plays it from there.
  • Every idea in the sentence is required: a scene that only shows part of it doesn't rank first. A word with a minus sign (-beach) is excluded.
  • The search learns: tick the right results, then "More like this" or "Less like this".
  • Similar videos: from any video, one switch turns the list into the 150 closest videos, across all folders.
  • Search by voice: a microphone in the search field; speech recognition runs on the PC too.
  • Nothing leaves your computer: the model runs on the PC, at low priority, and each video is analyzed only once. You can search what's already indexed while the rest carries on.
What Prisme doesn't do (or not yet)
  • It doesn't recognize people: no search by performer from their face, and no online metadata.
  • Search by description is in beta: often right, not always. First use downloads the libraries and the model, several gigabytes, once.
  • Automatic keywords are only as good as your file names.

Frequently asked questions

How do I tag porn videos automatically?

Start from the file names: the most frequent words become categories. Prisme does it without any typing, filtering out technical jargon, and lets you add your own keywords.

Can AI find a scene without sending my videos online?

Yes, with a model that runs on your PC, like the one in Prisme's AI Lab. Your video frames and your searches never leave the computer; only the model files are downloaded, once.

Do I need a graphics card?

No: the fast engine runs on an ordinary laptop's processor. An NVIDIA card speeds up indexing a big collection.
