A porn video player with hover previews, a video Wall and a floating player

A classic player, VLC or the one in Windows, opens a file and plays it from the start. For an adult video collection, you want the opposite: see right away what each video contains, jump to the right moment, watch several at once. Here is what a player built for that changes.

In short

Prisme shows what's in your videos before you even open them: ten previews on hover over a folder, five moments along each video. It plays up to ten videos at once on the Wall, and keeps playback on top of other windows with the floating player. All in the same software that sorts and files them.

Previews: know without opening

  • Hover over a folder: up to ten previews of its videos play on its thumbnail, sampled across the whole list, not just the first ones.
  • Hover over a thumbnail: the video starts at that moment and loops for a few seconds, muted by default.
  • In a video's view, five moments line up beside the picture; hover over one and playback jumps there. Hold Shift: nine moments cover the picture.
  • The scroll wheel skips forward or back in the hovered video, everywhere. Hovering over the progress bar shows the frame at that point.
  • Zoom up to ×6, centered under the mouse.

Previews are prepared ahead of time, in the background: a page of 40 thumbnails you've already seen comes back in 0.07 s, even on a NAS.

The Wall: up to ten videos at once

  • Two to ten videos playing together. Portrait and landscape mixed: the mosaic arranges itself to fill the screen.
  • Each video has its own player: pause, skip, favorite, or swap in another, without touching its neighbors. Sound follows the mouse.
  • When a video ends, another of the same shape replaces it. Two tiles never show the same video.
  • You sort from the Wall: a category's key files the hovered video, Del deletes it.
  • A double-click sends one video alone to full screen.

The floating player: on top of everything

The video moves into a frameless window, always on top, shaped like the picture. It opens by itself when Prisme is minimized or covered, or on demand with Ctrl+L. It resumes at the exact moment, and you can sort from it like anywhere else.

And the sound

Videos recorded too quietly are boosted automatically during playback, up to +30 dB, without ever lowering the ones that are already loud. Prisme's speaker has its own volume and a choice of output: headphones, speakers, the screen.

Discreet, even while playing

Videos play inside Prisme, leaving no recent-files list in another player. Ctrl+K replaces everything, videos included, with a dull screen (details).

What Prisme doesn't do
  • It doesn't stream videos from websites: it plays your files, on your PC, an external drive or a NAS.
  • It doesn't play VR in a headset.

Frequently asked questions

Which video player shows previews on hover?

Classic players (VLC, Windows Media Player) don't show them on a folder's files. Prisme plays ten previews on hover over a folder, and starts the video at the moment you hover.

How do I watch several videos at once?

With several VLC windows arranged by hand, or with Prisme's Wall, which shows two to ten, each with its own player, and replaces the ones that end by itself.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. An ordinary computer plays six to ten 1080p videos at once on the Wall. Prisme picks CPU or graphics card decoding by itself.
