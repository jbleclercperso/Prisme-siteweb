Porn duplicate finder: the same scene, even at another resolution

The same scene downloaded twice, once in 720p and then in 1080p, under two different names, on two drives: in an adult collection, duplicates are the rule. The usual tools only see identical copies. Here is how to find the others too, and keep the best version.

In short

Exact copies (same file, another name) are found by any free tool, like dupeGuru or Czkawka. The same scene re-encoded at another resolution has neither the same name, nor the same size, nor the same bytes: you have to compare the pictures.

Prisme does it across the whole collection at once, puts the best version first and never deletes anything without you.

Why duplicates pile up

  • The same scene published on several sites, under different titles.
  • A short cut, a long cut, a better-quality version downloaded later.
  • Copies made from one drive to another, then forgotten.

On a collection built over several years, they often weigh hundreds of gigabytes.

What Prisme does

Prisme is Windows software to sort, organize and watch an adult video collection. Its duplicate search finds, in one go:

  • Exact copies: same size down to the byte, then three passages of each file compared (start, middle, end). Two different parts that happen to share a size aren't mixed up.
  • Other encodes: the same scene in 480p, 720p, 1080p or 4K, recognized by its pictures, with duration as a safeguard. Two videos of clearly different lengths are never declared identical.
  • Across the whole collection: PC, external drive and NAS together.
  • Photos too: in Photos mode, the same search finds duplicate photos, even resized, recompressed or converted.

For each group:

  • The best version first, marked "✓ keep": highest resolution, then largest file, then longest. You can combine your own criteria and set preferred folders.
  • See before you decide: three frames of each copy, taken at the same moments and lined up, with resolution, size and duration. "Compare as video" plays them side by side.
  • Nothing is deleted without you: the extra copies are ticked, and you confirm. Deletion goes through the session trash, and Ctrl+Z brings it back. "Not duplicates" dismisses a group for good.
  • Fast afterwards: the first search is long on a big collection, the following ones are almost instant.
What Prisme doesn't do
  • It doesn't recognize a scene that is only an excerpt of another (a compilation, a trailer): durations must match.
  • It never deletes anything on its own.
  • On a NAS, which has no recycle bin, deleted files are erased for good when Prisme closes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the same video at different resolutions?

By comparing frames taken at the same moments in each file, and checking that durations match. Name and size are useless: a 720p version has almost nothing in common with the 1080p one, except its pictures.

Which version should I keep?

The highest resolution, then the heaviest file (the least compressed), then the longest. Prisme applies that order by default and lets you change it.

Do free tools find re-encoded duplicates?

Most only find exact copies. Czkawka has a "similar videos" mode that compares frames, and Stash has a fingerprint-based search. Prisme does both kinds in a single search, with a review screen built to decide fast.
