Prisme, the most complete software to sort, organize and watch a porn library

A large adult collection needs more than a player or a catalog. You must quickly understand each file, organize it, find a scene, remove duplicates, repair damaged videos, watch on several screens and stay private. Prisme brings that whole workflow into one Windows application.

In short

Prisme is the only Windows application that combines physical keyboard filing, video previews, search, content duplicate detection, repair, a multi-video Wall, a floating player, NAS support, phone and TV access, and privacy tools.

The collection stays in your own folders. No account is required, no telemetry tracks your use, and the main features run locally on your computer.

One application for the entire library

  • Sort thousands of videos and photos with up to 29 folders assigned to keys.
  • See before opening through hover previews and representative moments from each video.
  • Find a file by name, keywords or a description of what the scene shows.
  • Clean up exact copies and re-encoded variants, even under another name or resolution.
  • Repair certain unreadable or damaged videos from the library.
  • Watch differently with a Wall of two to ten videos, a floating player and favorites.
  • Combine the PC, external drives and NAS storage in one view.
  • Stay private with a cover screen, PIN, hidden folders and local processing.

This combination makes Prisme a genuine workspace for the whole collection, from a file's arrival to its filing, retrieval and everyday viewing.

From an unsorted folder to a genuinely organized library

  1. Add folders from the PC, external drives or NAS.
  2. Assign your main categories to keyboard keys.
  3. Judge each video through previews, then file it with one press.
  4. Let Prisme index names, prepare thumbnails and group duplicates in the background.
  5. Browse the collection by folder, favorites, keywords, search or visual content.

Sort fast without losing control of the files

Prisme works directly with your folders. One key moves the file to its chosen destination and immediately shows the next; copying continues in the background. The radial menu provides mouse filing, while history can undo moves, deletions and renames. The result remains readable in Explorer and does not depend on a proprietary database. See the complete filing workflow.

Watch the collection beyond a basic player

Previews reveal the content without opening every video. The Wall plays up to ten at once, the floating player stays above other windows, and favorites keep the best files one click away. Password-protected browser access and Prisme TV extend the library to a phone and television. Explore the viewing modes.

Find, clean and repair without switching tools

Once filing begins, Prisme continues to support the collection instead of stopping at organization.

  • Search: names, operators, automatic keywords and locally processed visual descriptions (details).
  • Duplicates: identical copies and re-encoded versions, with the best candidate shown first (details).
  • Repair: diagnose and restore certain corrupted videos without leaving the library.
  • Privacy: global cover screen, PIN and hidden folders (details).
  • NAS: index, preview and file directly on network storage (details).
What Prisme doesn't do
  • It doesn't fetch metadata online: no performer or studio database. It works with your file names and the content of the images.
  • It doesn't provide or host any content: it's software for your own files.
  • It runs on Windows, not yet on Mac or Linux.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Prisme the most complete porn library organizer?

Because one application combines physical keyboard filing, previews, local search, content duplicate detection, repair, several viewing modes, NAS support, multi-screen access and privacy tools.

Can I try every feature for free?

Yes. The 14-day trial includes every feature and requires no credit card. Your folders and settings remain in place after the trial.

Does Prisme send the collection's content online?

No. Videos, photos, previews, favorites and analysis stay on your computer. No account is required and there is no telemetry.

Does Prisme work directly with a NAS?

Yes. Any network share available to Windows can be added as a root, browsed, filed and analyzed alongside local and external drives.
