How to hide porn on your PC: what actually protects it

A shared PC, a relative borrowing the computer, someone walking in at the wrong moment: these are two different problems. Making sure nobody stumbles on your collection, and making sure nobody can find it by looking. Here is what works for each, from free Windows settings to encryption.

In short

A Windows "hidden" folder hides nothing from someone who looks. For real protection, use a Windows account of your own with a password, and for the files themselves, encryption: BitLocker, or a free VeraCrypt vault.

To avoid being caught while watching, you need one gesture that makes everything vanish instantly. Prisme has one: Ctrl+K replaces everything with a dull, animated screen, and only a PIN code brings the collection back.

What doesn't protect

  • The "hidden" attribute: tick "Hidden items" in File Explorer and everything is back.
  • A folder with a boring name ("Drivers", "Backup 2019"): it doesn't survive a search, or File Explorer's thumbnails.
  • A "protected" zip archive that you extract to watch: the extracted files stay on the disk.

The traces people forget

  • Recent files: File Explorer's Home page and the Start menu show what you opened. Untick "Show recently used files" in File Explorer's options, and turn off recent items in Settings › Personalization › Start.
  • Cloud sync: a folder inside OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox goes to their servers and to your other devices. Keep the collection out of synced folders.
  • The Photos app in Windows gathers pictures and videos from the Pictures and Videos folders: store the collection elsewhere.
  • Your player: many players keep a list of recently played files. VLC clears it in Tools › Preferences, by unticking "Save recently played items".

What actually protects

  1. A Windows account of your own, with a password or PIN. Other users of the PC can't open your personal folders. Simple, free, and enough in most households.
  2. Disk encryption: BitLocker (Windows Pro) or Device Encryption (on many Windows Home PCs) protects the whole disk if the PC is stolen or taken apart.
  3. An encrypted vault: VeraCrypt, free and open source, creates a vault file that mounts as a drive with a password and becomes unreadable again once closed. The strongest protection for a specific collection.

Frequently asked questions

How do I hide a folder on Windows 11?

Right-click › Properties › tick "Hidden": the folder disappears from File Explorer, but comes back as soon as hidden items are shown. For real protection, use a Windows account of your own, or an encrypted VeraCrypt vault.

How do I password-protect a folder?

Windows doesn't offer it directly. The safest option is a VeraCrypt vault (free): a file that mounts as a drive with a password. BitLocker, on Windows Pro, encrypts a whole drive or USB stick.

Is there a panic button to hide a window instantly?

Windows has Win+D, which shows the desktop, but one click brings everything back. Prisme has a real cover screen: Ctrl+K, or Ctrl+Alt+K from any window, and a PIN code to come back.

Does Prisme send anything online?

No. Prisme is fully local, with no account or telemetry. Sharing to your phone only opens if you turn it on, and it's password-protected.
