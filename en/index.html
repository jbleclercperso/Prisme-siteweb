Does Prisme send my videos anywhere?

No. Prisme runs entirely on your computer. Your videos, photos, favorites, searches and thumbnails never leave your machine, and there is no telemetry.

The only exceptions are the ones you choose: remote access, which only opens once you've set a password, goes through a secure address and can be switched off in one click; and web search, which only sends the words you search for, never anything from your collection.

Does Prisme modify my files?

Only when you ask it to: moving, renaming or discarding a video. Everything else (favorites, index, thumbnails) is stored separately, in the app's data. Only the session trash, a hidden folder, settles into your collection for the length of the session, and disappears when Prisme closes.

And every action can be undone.

My videos are on a NAS or an external drive. Does it work?

Yes, and that's exactly where Prisme makes the difference. It has been optimized and measured on collections stored on NAS drives: what it has already seen appears instantly, previews are prepared in advance, and the drive is only reread in the background.

Good to know: a NAS has no Windows Recycle Bin. What you discard there stays recoverable for the whole session, then is deleted for good when Prisme closes.

Can I watch from my phone?

Yes, from anywhere: set a password, scan the QR code, and browse your folders, search and watch your videos in your phone's browser. Nothing is downloaded to the phone.

Your PC does the streaming: it must stay on, without going to sleep, with Prisme open. The screen itself can turn off. If your videos are on a NAS that can run Docker, you can also, optionally, publish the library there: it then stays reachable with the PC off.

What about the TV?

Yes, with the Prisme TV app, for Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. On first launch, it finds Prisme on your network: you approve the TV with one click on the PC, and that's it. After that, it opens straight onto your library, to browse and watch with the remote.

Like any adult app, it isn't offered in the app stores: it installs in a few minutes from a file. See how to install it.

Which formats are supported?

For videos: MP4, M4V, MKV, MOV, AVI, WMV, FLV, WEBM, MPG, TS, M2TS, VOB, 3GP, OGV, RMVB, ASF, DivX and F4V. For photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, BMP and TIFF. Each file's resolution and orientation are detected automatically.

Can I download videos from my sites?

Yes. Prisme's search queries the sites you've saved and gathers their videos in a single window; one click on “Download” files them into your collection, in the best quality, including from sites that don't offer a download button. You can also paste the link of a video found elsewhere.

As with any download, you remain responsible for how you use it, in keeping with the sites' terms and their creators' rights.
